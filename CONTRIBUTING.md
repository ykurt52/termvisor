# Contributing

termvisor is developed by its maintainer and a small group of
collaborators.

## Pull requests

Pull requests are accepted only from collaborators of this repository.
Pull requests from anyone else are closed without review, however good
the change is. Please do not take it personally; it keeps the project
manageable.

## Becoming a collaborator

Write to the maintainer, Yunus Kurt, at yunuskkurt@gmail.com: who you
are, what you would like to work on and how you use termvisor.

## Bugs and ideas

Bug reports and feature requests are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/ykurt52/termvisor/issues). A good bug report
has:

- the termvisor version (menu → About termvisor → Troubleshooting has
  everything worth pasting),
- what you did, what you expected and what happened,
- for a session problem, the kind of session (serial, SSH, ...) and,
  where it helps, the log.

## Your own changes

termvisor is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later. You are welcome to
fork it and change it for yourself, within the terms of the license
(see [LICENSE](LICENSE)): a version you distribute must stay under the
same license, with its source available.
